Privacy policy

Tagwright is a photo-cataloguing app for Android. This page says exactly what it does with your data.

English Svenska

Applies to the Android app app.tagwright. Last updated 2026-10-20.

English

The short version

  • There is no server of ours. Your catalogue, your photos and your settings live on your phone.
  • There is no account and no sign-up. We cannot identify you, because we never receive anything about you.
  • Your photos go only to the AI provider you chose, with the key you entered — and only when you ask the app to catalogue something.
  • EXIF, GPS and XMP are stripped from every copy that is uploaded.
  • Advertising is the only third party, and it is disclosed below.

What the app collects and where it goes

WhatWhere it goes
Your photos Stays on your phone, except when you ask for metadata: then one copy is sent directly from your phone to the AI provider whose key you entered. We are not in that path and never see the image.
The metadata the model returns Stays on your phone, in a local database.
Your API key Stored in the Android Keystore, encrypted. It is never written to plain settings, never written to a log, and excluded from Android's backup.
Your templates and settings Stays on your phone. Android's own auto-backup is enabled, so these — like most Android apps' data — are copied to your own Google account, not to us. You control that in Android's settings.
A local error log A bounded, on-device log that helps you report a problem. It never leaves the phone and is deleted with the app.
Advertising data AdMob, only if ads are shown. See below.
Purchases Google Play Billing, for the optional Pro upgrade. We never see your payment details.

Advertising

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect a device or other identifier and use it to serve and measure ads. That is the only data collection that is not entirely under your control from inside the app, and it is why the app's Play data-safety declaration says that it collects data.

  • No ads ever appear in the camera flow. Not before a photo, not after one, not while the queue runs.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, a consent message appears before any personalised ad is loaded, and you can change your choice afterwards.
  • Pro removes ads entirely.

What the app does NOT do

  • No accounts, no login, no email address required.
  • No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No cloud sync and no backend of ours.
  • No API keys sold inside the app, and no link to an external payment page.
  • It never modifies, moves or overwrites your original photos.

Your AI provider is a separate business

When the app sends a photo for cataloguing, you are using that provider's service under their terms and their privacy policy, not ours. What they do with the request is between you and them. You choose the provider, you enter your own key, and you can remove it at any time.

Deleting your data

  • Settings → Delete all data removes your objects, photos and settings from the phone.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything the app kept, including the error log and the stored key.
  • There is nothing to delete on our side, because there is nothing on our side.

Children

The app is a tool for adults. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it, and the new version applies from that date.

Annonser

Gratisversionen visar annonser via Google AdMob. AdMob kan samla in en enhets- eller annan identifierare och använda den för att visa och mäta annonser. Det är den enda datainsamling som inte helt styrs inifrån appen, och skälet till att appens data-safety-deklaration säger att den samlar in data.

  • Inga annonser visas någonsin i kameraflytet. Inte före en bild, inte efter en, inte medan kön kör.
  • Inom EES, Storbritannien och Schweiz visas ett consent-meddelande innan någon personlig annons laddas, och du kan ändra ditt val efteråt.
  • Pro tar bort annonser helt.

Vad appen INTE gör

  • Inga konton, ingen inloggning, ingen e-postadress krävs.
  • Ingen analys, ingen telemetri, ingen kraschrapportering.
  • Ingen försäljning eller delning av dina data med någon.
  • Ingen molnsynk och ingen backend hos oss.
  • Inga API-nycklar säljs i appen, och ingen länk till en extern betalsida.
  • Den ändrar, flyttar eller skriver aldrig över dina originalbilder.

Din AI-leverantör är en egen verksamhet

När appen skickar en bild för katalogisering använder du den leverantörens tjänst under deras villkor och deras integritetspolicy, inte våra. Vad de gör med förfrågan är mellan dig och dem. Du väljer leverantör, du anger din egen nyckel, och du kan ta bort den när som helst.

Radera dina data

  • Inställningar → Radera all data tar bort dina objekt, bilder och inställningar från telefonen.
  • Att avinstallera appen tar bort allt appen sparat, inklusive felloggen och den lagrade nyckeln.
  • Det finns inget att radera hos oss, för det finns inget hos oss.

Barn

Appen är ett verktyg för vuxna. Den riktar sig inte till barn och samlar inte medvetet in något från dem.
